Frequently asked

How does Rootstock work?

Each “gate” teaches a small family of roots, then has you build the meanings of real words from those pieces. A gate tempers overnight before a final typed trial; once sealed, its words return on a spaced schedule so they stay with you. Clear all the gates and the Bar opens — a fifty-item examination — followed by the Drill Hall for open-ended practice.

What is free, and what needs a subscription?

Gates I–III are free forever. Rootstock Full opens all twenty-four gates, the Review Docket at full strength, the Bar, and the Drill Hall.

I already subscribed but the app shows me as free.

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. Restoring re-checks your entitlement with the App Store.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Rootstock and choose Cancel Subscription.

You keep access until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

On your old device, open Settings → Get a backup code and copy the code. On the new device, open Settings → Restore from a code and paste it. (A subscription itself restores separately via Restore purchases on the same Apple ID.)

Does Rootstock work offline?

Yes. Once installed, the app runs fully offline. Your progress is stored on your device.

What data do you collect about me?

None. Rootstock has no accounts and no tracking; your progress never leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy.
