Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026 · Last updated July 25, 2026

Rootstock is built to teach you words — not to learn about you. The app is designed to work without collecting your personal data.

This policy explains what Rootstock (“the app,” “we,” “us”) does and does not do with information. It applies to the Rootstock iOS app published by Gabriel Anderson.

The short version

  • We do not require an account, name, email, or sign-in.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Your learning progress is stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

As you use Rootstock, the app saves your progress — which gates you have sealed, your review schedule, your daily streak, your lexicon, and your settings — in local storage on your device. This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Rootstock can generate a backup code that encodes your progress so you can move it to another device. This code is created on your device and is only ever shared if you choose to copy and share it yourself. We do not receive or store backup codes.

Subscriptions and purchases

Rootstock offers an optional auto-renewable subscription (“Rootstock Full”). All purchases, billing, and renewals are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, payment card, or Apple ID. Apple’s handling of your purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

To unlock paid content, the app asks the device whether an active subscription exists and receives only a yes/no entitlement signal from Apple’s on-device StoreKit framework. This signal contains no personal identity.

No data leaves your device

Apart from Apple’s purchase processing described above, Rootstock does not send your information off your device. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social logins, or third-party trackers.

Children’s privacy

Rootstock is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request or delete on our servers.

Your choices

You can remove all locally stored progress at any time by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone).

Changes to this policy

If we release a future version that changes how information is handled — for example, adding optional cloud sync or diagnostics — we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and note the new effective date above.
