# The Dragon Codex — Art Direction

The Dragon Codex is an original fantasy presentation for Rootstock. Its visual
language is an illuminated scholarly manuscript: aged vellum, oxblood leather,
antiqued gold, muted forest green, ember red, engraved map lines, roots, vines,
and elegant original dragons. It does not use Dungeons & Dragons names, logos,
settings, characters, symbols, or copied artwork.

All final assets were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow,
then normalized to WebP for the bundled app. Shared constraints for every
prompt were: no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, dice, trademarked
symbols, recognizable franchise creatures, or imitation of a named artist.

## Asset prompts

### `frame-mobile.webp`

Create a tall illuminated-manuscript border around an aged vellum page. A long
serpentine dragon, botanical roots, dark red vines, gilt flourishes, compass
marks, constellation dots, and tiny bestiary sketches stay in narrow outer
edge bands. Keep the central 70 percent calm, pale, low-contrast, and empty for
mobile app content. Use warm candlelit vellum, oxblood leather, aged gold,
forest green, and ember red.

### `frame-wide.webp`

Create a wide manuscript environment for a narrow centered app column. Reserve
the central 44 percent as a quiet vellum reading area from top to bottom.
Concentrate original dragons, roots, gold vines, cartographic arcs, and tiny
bestiary marginalia in the far left and right oxblood-leather gutters.

### `dashboard-vignette.webp`

Create a wide oxblood-leather banner showing an elegant original dragon curled
around the exposed roots of an ancient tree. Keep the left-center 62 percent
dark and low-detail for cream-colored interface copy. Place the dragon and
brightest gilt detail at upper right, with roots framing the bottom while
leaving a clear progress-bar strip.

### `interior-marginalia.webp`

Create a pale horizontal vellum folio with delicate dragon-wing curves,
botanical roots, map arcs, stars, gold-leaf corners, one tiny sleeping dragon,
and one tiny antlered bestiary sketch around the outer 12 percent. Keep the
central 82 percent empty and low-contrast for dense study text.

### `dragon-crest.webp`

Create a centered circular illuminated medallion on dark oxblood leather. An
original dragon curls around the roots of a small ancient tree to form a bold,
readable heraldic seal. Use engraved gold, muted forest green, parchment cream,
and ember red, with generous dark padding outside the medallion.
