Why this name. “Rootstock” is short, brandable, on-theme (a rootstock is the root system a plant is grafted onto), and distinctive enough to rank once indexed. “Word Power” stays as the in-app tagline and a keyword — it is not in the store name, which sidesteps any association with the Norman Lewis / Reader’s Digest “Word Power” marks.

Before you commit: search the App Store and the USPTO TESS for “Rootstock” in apps/education. If taken, fallbacks that keep the brand: Rootstock: Word Roots, Rootstock Vocabulary, Wordstock: Roots. Reserve the name in App Store Connect early — it locks it for you.

05App Privacy (“Data Not Collected”)

With the recommended build (no analytics/ads SDK, fonts bundled locally, purchases via Apple), you can honestly select “Data Not Collected” in the App Privacy questionnaire. Answers:

  • Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? → No (i.e., “Data is not collected”).
  • Tracking (ATT)? → No tracking. No NSUserTrackingUsageDescription, no IDFA.
  • This yields a clean “Data Not Collected” privacy label — a genuine selling point for this app.
Conditional: if you later add RevenueCat, Firebase, or any analytics/crash SDK, you must update these answers (typically “Purchases” and “Identifiers/Diagnostics” linked to app functionality/analytics) and revise the Privacy Policy. Keep v1 SDK-free to keep the label pristine.
